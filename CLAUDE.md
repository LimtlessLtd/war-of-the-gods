# Public repo boundary

This repo is public and holds only `Website/` plus its guards (see `GIT_SETUP.md`). DM-only material (notes, handouts, gods, PCs, Discord export, maps, the private DM site) lives in the same folder on disk but is never committed: keep it that way, and never copy DM-only content into `Website/`.

# Campaign website content rules

- The session recordings and their transcripts (`Website/content/transcripts.json`) are the source of truth. Moment blurbs, tags and quotes describe only what can be heard in the clip; attribute a quote to a character only when the transcript makes the speaker clear.
- Only include Discord posts and images that relate to the D&D sessions being played at the time, or that refer to something that happened in the sessions. Leave out real-life photos, generic memes and anything unrelated.
- Workflow for new sessions and moments is in `Website/README.md`.
