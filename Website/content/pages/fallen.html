Nathaniel Wavecrest

Hero of Muttonham. Architect of the waterfall. Last of the Wavecrests.

Played by
Eugenio
Fell
Session 33 · Day 46 · 16th of Wolfmoon
Where
A haunted house on Mt Mistmourne
Time since landing
46 days

Human warlock, pirate, and one of the last of a sailing dynasty chosen by the Mad Sea Mother. He was marked by Blibdoolpoolp at the river crossing, murdered his way through Campess Port, blew up a church and paid for everybody's beer. At Muttonham he double-crossed Auril herself and sent a ship over the waterfall onto three hundred Frost Wardens, and the whole town chanted his name. Auril's blight crept up his chest after that, and he died once in the Nogratis caves and got better. The second time, walking into a haunted house with Lyrial, he didn't.

Lyrial Thorne

Orphan, smuggler, bard, and Grub's best friend.

Played by
Chris
Fell
Session 33 · Day 46 · 16th of Wolfmoon
Where
The same haunted house, beside Nate
Time since landing
46 days

An unwanted child from Dryton, raised in a Reeding orphanage, who learned to hide in plain sight moving goods through the docks. He came to Campess Port chasing rumours of an orphan causing havoc. Agni marked him, Grub adored him, and he once spat on Auril's shrine and nearly died of it. He applied to lecture at Nogratis University, disappeared after the jailbreak with only a stone-message left behind, and made his last stand in a house full of ghosts.

Gavin

Came to rescue a prisoner. The prisoner killed him. Twice.

Played by
Graham
Fell
Session 39 · Day 55 · 25th of Wolfmoon
Where
A pit in Fireholm Prison
Time since landing
55 days

A human fighter who joined in Fireholm with one goal: get a certain prisoner out of Fireholm Prison. The prisoner was Fiddle. Gavin turned out to be a bit of a dick, so the team settled it the only way they know, with a pit fight. Fiddle won, resurrected Gavin, and won again. Fiddle's first quest, Saving Private Fiddle, was technically Gavin's last request.

Tijuana the Iguana

A long and stable member of the group.

Companion of
Ulrick
Fell
The airship days
Cause
Punched, then thrown off an airship

An iguana from a loot table who lived in the nook between Ulrick's shield and breastplate, head poking over one shoulder, and ate maggots from the magic box. For a while she and a jar of tadpoles served as Nate's proof of identity. Nobody talks about how she ended up over the side, but the table knows who did it.
