People of Aurilia

Friends, enemies, and the many people who were both at different times.

Allies

Cassandra Vulx

Ashen Brotherhood

Leader of the Campess Port chapter. She first appeared in Durzo's bath. Her aid arrived at Muttonham just when all seemed lost, and she is still in love with Sir Jared.

Sir Jared of Blampsfield

Muttonham

Broken out of Muttonham prison in Session 10, and the voice of Muttonham's defiance: "For Muttonham, for Agni, for freedom!"

Michael Flameaxes

Ashen Brotherhood

Rebuilding the Fireholm Brotherhood in the sewers. Gave Ulrick the Nimbus Coronet. The man the White Judgement dropped on the deck may not really be him.

Grub the Ethereal

Nyxara-touched

A goblin with a magic box and a tunnel network, bonded to Lyrial. He can materialise lost goblin loot.

Mori the Mortuarian

Nyxara's

Ferryman and seller of burial plans. He has turned up at the worst moments ever since Mt Mistmourne. Does he have foresight? Has he been round the wheel before?

Astrarius

Agni's faithful

The muscular tiefling leader of a nomadic band of Agni worshippers. She retook Frostfall Fortress with the team's help.

Farmer John

Campess Port

Lost his animals to spider-things and his legs to a hatchling. He now rolls into town in a wheelchair.

Mega-Jon

Gaia's

Tree Jon (blind), Bone Jon (mute) and Water Jon (deaf), combined. He threw his axe away at the Battle of Muttonham and turned up again on the Cinderflow.

King Flippy Nips

Guh-Nome-Home

Ruler of a gnome town with chest-high walls. No tall-ees allowed. Be home before tea time.

Teller

Fortune teller

Knows everyone's name, takes payment in shoes, shadows and secrets, and sees things nobody wants to hear.

R.E.D. & Vallos

D.E.R.E.K.'s family

A red construct brother and the inventor who built them both, living in a sealed lab full of vats.

The crew of the Skycrest
