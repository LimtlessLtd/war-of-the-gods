The Fallen

Heroes who did not make it to the end of the war, and the long list of people and creatures who met the heroes and regretted it.

Nathaniel Wavecrest

Hero of Muttonham. Architect of the waterfall. Last of the Wavecrests.

Played by
Eugenio
Fell
Session 33 · Day 46 · 16th of Wolfmoon
Where
A haunted house on Mt Mistmourne
Time since landing
46 days

Human warlock, pirate, and one of the last of a sailing dynasty chosen by the Mad Sea Mother. He was marked by Blibdoolpoolp at the river crossing, murdered his way through Campess Port, blew up a church and paid for everybody's beer. At Muttonham he double-crossed Auril herself and sent a ship over the waterfall onto three hundred Frost Wardens, and the whole town chanted his name. Auril's blight crept up his chest after that, and he died once in the Nogratis caves and got better. The second time, walking into a haunted house with Lyrial, he didn't.

Lyrial Thorne

Orphan, smuggler, bard, and Grub's best friend.

Played by
Chris
Fell
Session 33 · Day 46 · 16th of Wolfmoon
Where
The same haunted house, beside Nate
Time since landing
46 days

An unwanted child from Dryton, raised in a Reeding orphanage, who learned to hide in plain sight moving goods through the docks. He came to Campess Port chasing rumours of an orphan causing havoc. Agni marked him, Grub adored him, and he once spat on Auril's shrine and nearly died of it. He applied to lecture at Nogratis University, disappeared after the jailbreak with only a stone-message left behind, and made his last stand in a house full of ghosts.

The Heroes

They started as the Frostfall Four. The roster has changed a lot since then. Open a hero for their top three epic and funny moments, what they said at the table, and their story session by session.

The Gods

A thousand years ago Auril and Nyxara cast Agni down. Now fire is rising again, and every god wants a champion.

Agni

The Humble Axeman · Fire, warmth, justice
"As frosts thaw. Fires rise. Shadows grow. Tides turn."

Revealed himself in Campess Port, Session 6. He marked Ulrick, now his Harbinger of Judgement, and Lyrial. His Scales were stolen ages ago and reclaimed at the source of the Cinderflow. One of his avatars is trapped in the Nimbus Coronet.

Agni's revealHymn: Agni Flame Eternal

Auril

Lady Frostkiss · Ice, winter, tyranny
"Pledge yourselves to me, and you will know what it is to rule."

The ruler of Aurilia for a thousand years, backed by the Frost Wardens and the Winter's Legion. Nate carried her mark and betrayed her at Muttonham, and paid for it with a creeping blight.

Auril's revealHymn: Auril, All Hail!

Nyxara

The Veiled Shadow · Death, shadow, secrets
"All flames will die. All frosts will thaw. All seas will dry."

Found in a sealed temple beneath Campess Port. She helped Auril topple Agni, and Auril then betrayed her. She holds Durzo's sister Elara, named Durzo her Emissary, and has asked Agni for an alliance. Gideon carries her mark too.

Nyxara's revealHymn: Vow to the Veiled Reaper

Blibdoolpoolp

The Mad Sea Mother · Madness, sea, chaos
"Where is the thrill in the expected?"

Revealed herself in the river ruins, Session 7. She has toyed with the Wavecrest family for generations. She marked Nate, and now Fiddle is her chosen, with dice that have twenty-one sides.

Blibdoolpoolp's revealHymn: Doolmara Vask

Gaia

Nature's Shepherd · Nature, balance, life
"The world is sick. It has lost its balance."

She waits to rebuild whatever the war leaves behind. Her druids pointed the team to Mega-Jon, and her embrace marks Gideon, who restored life at the end of time.

Gaia's revealHymn: Stone & Seed

Chronos

The Timekeeper · Time, fate, destiny
"Let's start at the end. I have seen your deaths."

Met by Ulrick and Lyrial aboard a ship frozen in time, beside a grandfather clock that stops the world. His sigil marks the whole party, and the Temporal Anomaly showed them four possible endings.

Chronos's revealHymn: A Timeless Hymn
